## 2. New findings (parts and circuits added or changed since the first pass)

### BLOCKER

**AB-B1. `POE_LOSS_N` detector asserts continuously in DCM and in pulse-skip mode — i.e. at the amplifier's normal idle.**
- File: `B-poe-loss-detect.md` (circuit items 3–5, "Behaviour by case" rows 2 and 4). Datasheet: NCP4306 p.7–8 (turn-off at zero current; DRV low with no conduction), SN74LVC1G123 p.1/4 (B rising-edge trigger).
- Problem: "pulse present" P is defined as SW_SEC > 16 V. SW_SEC is the inductor input. Whenever both SRs are off and the inductor current is zero — every DCM dead time (Io < ΔIL/2 = 0.17–0.28 A, i.e. below 8–13 W, which is the amp-idle load with the gateware auto-idle of `decisions.md`), and the whole of every pulse-skip interval (ms after regen or a load dump, §8.3.5) — the node sits at VBUS ≈ 48 V through the inductor. SWDIV = 48/32.15 = 1.49 V: P = 1 (> 0.497 V) and L = 1 (< 2.495 V), so LA = 1 for hundreds of ns to milliseconds, far longer than the 165–307 ns qualifier. The monostable fires every cycle and `POE_LOSS_N` stays low; the FPGA latches it and mutes (R-34). The "no pulses ⇒ nothing to qualify" argument is wrong because P is a level, not a pulse. Same mechanism gives a false `ACF_PULSE` (AB-M3).
- Fix: gate "pulse present" on actual transformer conduction, not on SW_SEC level: P′ = forward-SR NCP4306 DRV high (10 V gate through 10 k/4.99 k into the AND, or a third comparator on the forward-SR drain < 5 V; TLV3204 instead of TLV3202). DRV_fwd is low in every dead time and in skip mode, so LA = P′·L is zero there and the plateau test is only made while the secondary is being driven. Redo the qualifier timing (DRV rises 30–60 ns after the pulse starts, so L is already settled) and the behaviour table; add "light-load DCM, 57 V in" and "skip after a regen step" to the bench cases. Also consider centring the threshold at 40.0 V (Rbot 6.49 k; band 39.4–40.6 V): the present worst unit trips at 41.0 V while a compliant Class 8 PI minimum of 41.1 V less bridge and jack drops is ≈ 41.0 V at VPRI (m-AB7).

### MAJOR

**AB-M1. SYNC pin driven above its absolute maximum, and driven while VREF is off.**
- File: `B-sync-clock.md` (chain item 1, 4), `B-controller.md` SYNC row. Datasheet: UCC2897A p.5 abs max "FB, CS, SYNC, LINEOV, LINEUV: −0.3 V to VREF + 0.3 V"; p.6 VREF 4.75–5.25 V over temperature; §8.3.1.4 "The 5-V bias is available only when the UVLO circuit enables the operation" and §8.3.1.16 "When VDD reaches 12.7 V … 5 V appears on the VREF terminal". HT7550-1 output 4.85–5.15 V (UMW table, ±3 %); 74HC4017 VOH ≈ VCC at light load.
- Problem: (a) steady state: a 5.15 V clock into a part whose VREF may be 4.75 V exceeds VREF + 0.3 V = 5.05 V. (b) start-up and every UVLO event: the clock chain runs from VB_PRI ≈ 2–3 V upward, but VREF is 0 V until VDD passes 12.2–13.2 V (and after it falls below 7.6–8.4 V). During the VB_PRI ramp (and the LINEUV/UVLO soft-stop cases) Q0 drives up to 5 V into a pin whose abs max is then 0.3 V, through only 100 Ω: ≈ 40 mA pulses into the input clamp on every power-up.
- Fix (preferred): power the LVC1GU04, crystal and 74HC4017 from the UCC2897A **VREF** (5 V, "approximately 5 mA", p.4/§8.3.1.4) and delete the HT7550. The logic high can then never exceed VREF, and the clock exists only when the controller is alive. Budget: 74HC4017 ≈ CPD·V·f ≈ 1.1 mA, LVC1GU04 Pierce at 5 V ≈ 1–2 mA, FB pull-up 0.5 mA ⇒ ≈ 3–3.5 mA of 5 mA; measure at bring-up (a 3.3 V-rail option does not exist on the primary). Fallback: keep the LDO, add a 1 kΩ series resistor and a BAT54 from SYNC to VREF, and accept the clamp at the abs-max line. Either way CVREF should move to 0.22 µF (recommended, §8.3.1.4) with the extra load.

**AB-M2. The "clock-failure fallback" is not guaranteed: a stopped clock can hold SYNC high and switch the converter off.**
- File: `B-sync-clock.md` ("Clock-failure fallback"), `B-interfaces.md` ("If the clock fails the controller free-runs"), `B-converter-design.md` item 1. Datasheet: UCC2897A §8.3.6 "both outputs of the UCC2897A controller are off if the synchronization signal stays continuously high"; 74HC4017 Table 3 (MR high ⇒ Q0 = HIGH; counter freezes with no clock).
- Problem: if the crystal or inverter stops, the 74HC4017 freezes in its last state. In one state of ten Q0 is HIGH, SYNC stays high, and the converter is dead (no free-running fallback); the unbuffered inverter's mid-rail output with a dead crystal can also leave CP0 at an undefined level. The audit's 72 Vµs fallback analysis is right for the other nine states; the claim "the converter keeps working" is not. Also: MR (pin 15) and CP1 (pin 13) are not assigned anywhere in the audit; both must be tied (CP1 low; MR low or as below).
- Fix: take the SYNC pulse from **Q1** (same 136 ns width, one count later) and drive **MR** from a clock-loss detector on the 7.3728 MHz node (BAT54S charge pump, 1 nF, 100 kΩ to GND: MR held low while the clock runs, rises within ≈ 0.1 ms when it stops). Reset forces Q1–Q9 LOW (Table 3), SYNC low, and the controller free-runs at 590–708 kHz as intended. Alternatively, state honestly that a stopped clock may stop the converter and drop the fallback claim from the three files.

**AB-M3. `ACF_PULSE` does not mean "one pulse per converter cycle", so block E's clock-fault rule (±0.5 % over a 10 ms count) mutes the amplifier in normal operation.**
- Files: `B-poe-loss-detect.md` item 3 (`ACF_PULSE` = P), `B-interfaces.md` (`ACF_PULSE` row and addendum), `E-interfaces.md:138`, `D-interfaces.md:171`.
- Problem: with P = SW_SEC > 16 V, DCM gives two rising edges per cycle (the forward pulse, and the node springing to VBUS after the freewheel SR opens) and skip mode gives none for milliseconds. A 10 ms count therefore reads up to 2× or several % low at idle or after a loud passage — outside ±0.5 % — and E's hard rule declares a converter clock fault and mutes.
- Fix: derive `ACF_PULSE` from the gated P′ of AB-B1 (exactly one edge per real pulse), and change the gateware test from a count to a **period test**: in synchronised operation every pulse-to-pulse interval is an integer multiple of 1.3563 µs (±0.5 %), including skipped cycles; a free-running controller (656 kHz ± 10 %) fails that test within a few pulses. Record the new rule in `B-interfaces.md`, `E-interfaces.md` and `D-interfaces.md`.

### NOTE

- **n-AB1.** DMAX in sync: the datasheet (§8.3.1.5, §8.3.6) says the sync edge ends the charge and the discharge follows at the same slope to the same valley, so "DMAX is the same in free-running and synchronized modes" (the RON/ROFF ratio, 0.715 nominal), not 1 − tOFF/TSYNC = 0.68.
