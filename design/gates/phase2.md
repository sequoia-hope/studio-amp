## Reviews

Fresh-context reviewers on the stronger model:

| Review | First pass | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Visual (`review-phase2-visual.md`) | 0 BLOCKER, 6 MAJOR: root page was a table of contents; text collisions on rails, iso_dcdc and audio_out; DNP crosses hiding values; unnamed connector pins | all fixed by the owners and the lead (root redrawn as a block diagram with flow lines); re-check 8.5/10 PASS; 3 MINORs accepted (CC1/CC2 labels where a wire would cross D±; A2 head-room on mcu/fpga; rotated passive values) |
| Parts A–D (`review-phase2-parts-AD.md`) | 0 BLOCKER, 2 MAJOR: **T201 footprint mirrored** (would have bridged the isolation barrier in one orientation; the row spacing was also wrong); short-detector ladder gave ±0.81 V, not ±1.0 V | footprint rebuilt from the top view, checked pad by pad, pin-1 mark added, 18.1 mm barrier gap; ladder 4.99k/15k/4.99k (trip 14.8 A); minors fixed; verified CLOSED |
| Parts E–G (`review-phase2-parts-EFG.md`) | **1 BLOCKER: two BAV99s shorted −15 V to GND** (pin 3 tied to GND); 1 MAJOR: jack ferrites bypassed | BAV99 pin 3 no-connect, every dual diode's centre pin checked; beads in series; minors fixed; verified CLOSED |

Integration findings fixed before review: 28 decoupler style errors (amp sheets), 181 off-grid points
(FPGA sheet unit origin), a dangling label, the missing GND PWR_FLAG, two audited parts not drawn
(Fly-Buck snubber DNP, USB shell RC), and stale board-only fields (sync check).

**Lesson from this phase:** both the BAV99 short and the mirrored footprint passed ERC and the style
check. The BAV99 short came from a wire end landing on a pin by coordinate coincidence, which a
generated schematic is prone to. Netlist tracing against the datasheets is what caught both; keep it
as a standing part of every schematic review here.
