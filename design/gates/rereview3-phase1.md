## 2. New findings

### MAJOR
- **MAJOR-1. `B-vbus-droop.md` Circuit 3–4 (output stage).** LM393 onsemi EC table: **VOL ≤ 400 mV at 25 °C, ≤ 700 mV over temperature** (ISink ≤ 4 mA; here 1 mA). AO3400A p.2: **VGS(th) 0.65–1.45 V at 250 µA, 25 °C**, falling ≈ 2–3 mV/°C, i.e. ≈ 0.45 V min near 100 °C junction. In the *released* state the gate sits at the LM393's VOL; at the datasheet corners (0.7 V gate, 0.45 V threshold) the FET conducts tens of mA and pulls `POE_LOSS_N` (4.7 kΩ to +3V3, 0.7 mA) low: a **false PoE-loss mute and relay drop in normal hot operation**, the failure class this redesign exists to remove. At 25 °C limits (0.4 V vs 0.65 V) the leakage is ≈ 1 µA, so it will not show on a bench at room temperature. **Fix:** replace the AO3400A by a **2N7002** (VGS(th) 1.0–2.5 V; RDS(on) ≤ 7.5 Ω at 4.5 V → 10 mV at 1.37 mA, inside block F's ≤ 0.1 V) **and** change the 1 MΩ gate pull-down to **10 kΩ**, so the gate sees ≤ VOL/2 ≤ 0.35 V low and ≈ 4.9 V high. The output-high level then becomes ≈ 5 V, which moves the release point: with the 1.00 MΩ feedback it is 2.495 + 2.5 × 10 k/1.01 M = 2.520 V → **40.3 V, hysteresis 0.8 V**; either accept that (still > assert, > ripple) or lower the feedback resistor to ≈ 499 kΩ to restore ≈ 1.6 V, and update rows 2 of the shared rule in B/D/E and the E-pinmap A6 row.

### MINOR

The "2–4 µs" claim is optimistic; use **470 pF** (tw ≈ 4.5 µs; fallback still ≤ 5 µs) or keep 220 pF with the bench item made mandatory.

### NOTE
- n-1. Do not add a capacitor on the TL431 reference/cathode node (the 10 kΩ → + input and 1 MΩ are its only loads): 1 nF–1 µF is the TL431's unstable region; it is stable bare.
- n-2. LM5013 tON 984 ns at the 27.2 V UVLO-off corner is 1.6 % under the 1000 ns ROC with an unspecified tON tolerance; it only occurs during the last ms of a shutdown ramp. Record it.
- n-3. `B-vbus-droop.md` "Block F's relay OR keeps its relays off until 41.1 V" is true for VBUS ≳ 7.5 V (VSR present); below that, and on USB-only power, `POE_LOSS_N` reads high and the relays rely on the HP_EN/LO_EN gateware default, which is already the design.
- n-4. The ADC detector (40.5 V calibrated) trips before the comparator (≤ 40.5 V) in the calibrated case; the comparator is the FPGA-independent hardware path for block F's D_PL and the ADC-failure case. Both intended; say so.
