## Mode decision: PWM mode (single input with programmable dead time)

Chosen: **PWM mode**, DHL and DLH each to VSS through a resistor (Fig. 16).
- **Pins:** 1 PWM per half-bridge (4 in all) instead of HI + LI (8). EN is driven by the per-channel protection latch (D-ocp-logic.md), not directly by the FPGA.
- **Shoot-through is impossible by construction.** HO and LO are generated inside the chip with resistor-set dead times, so an FPGA timing bug cannot turn both FETs on. In IIM, "the controller is responsible for assuring that the LI and HI on-times do not overlap" (§8.2.2.4).
- **Edge fidelity.** In PWM mode the HS/LS delay matching is internal, and the FPGA's ≤ 1 ns edge placement (R-53) reaches the switch node unchanged except for the fixed propagation delay (11 ns typ / 21 ns max, p.7). IIM adds the 3.4 ns HS/LS mismatch to every edge.
- **Cost:** the dead time is fixed per build by resistor (tolerance below), so the FPGA cannot do adaptive dead time. Dead-time *compensation* (shifting the PWM edge by the known td when the edge is hard switched) is still possible in gateware and is required for open-loop THD; see Open questions.
- The mode is "sampled at power up and cannot be changed during operation" (§7.4). DLH must therefore never be tied to VDD.

## Required external parts (from datasheet)
All values and their calculations are in D-driver-passives.md and D-bootstrap.md.
- VDD–VSS: "at least 0.3 µF at bias, ESR < 500 mΩ" (§7.3.2, p.5 COUT); TI also recommends **100 nF close to the pins plus 220 nF–10 µF** (§9), and VDD cap ≥ 5 × CBST (§8.2.2.1) → 100 nF 0402 + 2.2 µF 0603.
- VIN–VSS: local bypass "as close as possible" (§9) → 1 µF 25 V 0603.
- HB–HS bootstrap cap (Eq. 7) → 220 nF 0603.
- External bootstrap diode BST→HB, rated ≥ the GaN VDS max, Schottky for high frequency, low capacitance (§8.2.2.2) → BAT46WJ.
- DHL and DLH resistors to VSS, 20 kΩ–1.8 MΩ (p.4 RDHL/RDLH) → 49.9 kΩ each.
- NC1 (pin 14) "should be either unconnected or tied to HS" → tie to HS. Pins 1, 11, 15 NC.
- Inputs: series resistor near the pins for ground-bounce filtering (§8.2.2.3) → 100 Ω, plus an optional 10 pF (DNP).

## Calculations

### Dead time (Eq. 4, 5; p.13)
Rext = 900/td − 25 (kΩ, ns). Chosen **td = 12 ns** for both DHL and DLH: Rext = 900/12 − 25 = 50.0 kΩ → **49.9 kΩ 1 %**, giving td = 900/(49.9 + 25) = 12.02 ns.
Check with Eq. 5: VDT = 1.8 V × 49.9/(49.9 + 25) = 1.199 V → td = (1.8 − 1.199) × 20 = 12.0 ns ✓.
Tolerance: the datasheet specifies only the ends (20 kΩ: 16–26 ns; 1.78 MΩ: −0.55 to 3.1 ns). At a mid setting the spread is roughly ±25–30 % of td, so 12 ns becomes ≈ 8.5–15.5 ns. The loss model covers 8–16 ns: idle 2.7–1.4 W for both channels. Dead time must stay well above the GaN turn-off time: LO/HO sink 3 A into Qg 6.1 nC → < 2 ns plus 21 ns max propagation, which is matched inside the chip. 8.5 ns ≥ 4× the turn-off time ✓, no shoot-through.
Why 12 ns (trade, D-gan-fet.md): longer dead time lowers idle loss (16 ns: 1.4 W vs 2.0 W) but raises the uncompensated dead-time error. Per leg that error is VBUS·td·fsw = 48 × 12 ns × 480 kHz = 0.28 V, and 0.55 V differentially, which as a square wave following sign(I) gives roughly H3 ≈ (4/π)(0.55/3)/28.3 V = 0.83 % at 50 W into 8 Ω (1.1 % at 16 ns). R-51 asks ≤ 0.3 % at 50 W, so gateware dead-time compensation is required either way, and its residual scales with td. The resistors are 0402, so td can be changed per build (R-53 "tunable per build").
DT resistor quiescent current (Eq. 8): 1.8 V/(25 k + 49.9 k) = 24 µA per pin.

### Driver supply current and VDRV load (Eq. 9, 10; p.5–6)
Per driver at fsw, with Qg max 7.9 nC (IGB070S10S1):
- Gate: 2 × Qg × fsw = 2 × 7.9 nC × 480 kHz = 7.58 mA
- Dynamic: ILSDyn 1.25 mA/MHz max × 0.48 = 0.60 mA; IHSDyn 0.7 mA/MHz max × 0.48 = 0.34 mA
- Quiescent: IDD 0.55 mA max (PWM mode) + IHB 0.85 mA max
- DT resistors 2 × 24 µA = 0.05 mA; gate pull-downs 10 kΩ at 5 V × D ≈ 0.25 mA each side → 0.5 mA
- **Total ≈ 10.4 mA per driver at 480 kHz; 4 drivers = 42 mA.** At 1 MHz (top of the R-53 range): 2 × 7.9 × 1 + 1.25 + 0.7 + 1.4 + 0.05 + 0.5 = 19.7 mA per driver, **79 mA for 4**.
- **VDRV request to block C: 7.0 V ± 5 % (any 6.0–18 V works; 7 V minimises LDO loss), ≤ 100 mA** (79 mA at 1 MHz + 25 % margin). Power from VDRV: 7 V × 42 mA = 0.29 W at 480 kHz (0.55 W at 1 MHz). R-33a budgeted 1.0 W.
- LDO headroom: dropout 750 mV max at 100 mA (p.5); at 20 mA it is much less. VIN(min) 6.65 V − 5.25 V = 1.4 V ✓.

### Driver dissipation and Tj
P_IC ≈ VIN × I − (gate energy dissipated in Rg_ext + Rg_int). Upper bound 7.35 V × 10.4 mA = 76 mW at 480 kHz (145 mW at 1 MHz). RθJA 40.5 °C/W (p.5) → ΔTj ≤ 3.1 °C (5.9 °C at 1 MHz). Tj ≈ board 63–83 °C + 6 → ≤ 90 °C < 125 °C ✓. The low-side over-temperature threshold is 150 °C min (§7.3.4).

### Start-up timing (gateware requirement for block E)
- tSTLS (PWM mode) 150 µs max after VDD passes UVLO; tSTHS 28 µs max after HB−HS passes UVLO (p.7).
- The bootstrap pre-charges through the internal 1 kΩ (§7.3.1), then refreshes each time LO is on.
- Sequence: VDRV good → wait ≥ 200 µs → latch armed, EN high with PWM low (all LS on, so BTL output = 0 V differential, pop-free) for ≥ 50 µs → start modulation at 50 % duty (BD idle).

### Minimum pulse and duty limits
- tPW (min input pulse that changes the output) is 4 ns max; pulses shorter than tPW,ext (up to 10 ns) are stretched to it (p.7). In PWM mode each output pulse is also shortened by td.
- Gateware limit: each leg's high and low times ≥ td(max) + 20 ns ≈ 36 ns → duty 1.7–98.3 % at 480 kHz. The bootstrap refresh needs LO on long enough to recharge CBST (D-bootstrap.md): ≥ 60 ns → **duty clamp 3–97 %** (M_max = 0.94).

## Fine print / gotchas
- "The part does not have an actual clamp on the high-side bootstrap supply" (§7.3.1): VBST = VDD − VF + IL·Rds(on). Checked in D-bootstrap.md: 5.21 V worst case, inside VHB−VHS 5.25 V recommended and the GaN 5.5 V recommended.
- DHL/DLH voltages pass an internal 10 kHz RC filter (§7.3.3). Do not put capacitance on these pins, and keep switching noise off them.
- The two thermal pads are on different dies with "very limited thermal coupling between a die and the opposite thermal pad" (§8.2.2.5). The VSS pad goes to the GND plane with vias; the HS pad goes to switch-node copper. "Design the thermal cooling of the board in a way to make the low-side die hotter" (§7.3.4), so the low side's OTP trips first and shuts both sides.
- Do's/Don'ts (§8.3): ≥ 4-layer board with the power return on an inner layer; small SMD bypass caps; "Don't drive the IC from a controller with a separate ground connection than the VSS pin of the IC, unless connecting through a CMC". **Our VSS is the GND plane at the LS source, with no shunt in the LS source** (current sensing is in the output path, D-current-sense.md). The FPGA is on the same solid GND plane (architecture decision 5), so the 100 Ω series input resistors are enough. Footprints for an optional CMC are not needed.
- "Pin transients above 200 V" must not occur: far above our 100 V GaN limit.
- The LMG1210 has **no overcurrent or desat protection** of its own. The OCP is external (D-current-sense.md, D-ocp-logic.md).
- Rev D raised IHSDyn from 0.61 to 0.7 mA/MHz; 0.7 is used above.
- MSL2 per the orderable addendum (Level-2-260C-1 YEAR); the text says MSL3. JLC handles either.

## Layout guidance from datasheet (§10)
- Driver as close to the GaN FETs as possible; HO/LO-to-gate traces as short as possible.
- Bootstrap diode as close as possible, so the BST-to-HB loop is small.
- Bypass VIN–VSS, VDD–VSS and HB–HS as close to the pins as possible; **VDD–VSS is a higher priority than VIN–VSS**.
- Separate power traces from signal traces; minimise overlap between layers; minimise capacitance from high-side pins to the input pins.
- Fig. 21 layout example: copy the placement pattern in phase 6.

## Open questions
1. **Dead-time compensation in gateware (block E):** required to meet R-51 open-loop THD. It needs the per-leg current sign: the inductor ripple plus the load current from the I-sense ADC (D-isense-adc.md), or a model. It also needs per-board calibration of the actual td (8.5–15.5 ns spread). Recommend populating the optional post-filter sense (D-postfilter-sense.md) on all five prototypes for that calibration.
2. td 12 ns is the baseline; the bench should measure idle loss and THD at 8, 12 and 16 ns.
