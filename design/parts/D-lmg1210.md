## Mode decision: PWM mode (single input with programmable dead time)
- **Pins:** 1 PWM per half-bridge (4 in all) instead of HI + LI (8). EN is driven by the per-channel fault latch (D-ocp-logic.md).
- **Shoot-through is impossible by construction:** HO/LO are generated inside the chip with resistor-set dead times. In IIM "the controller is responsible for assuring that the LI and HI on-times do not overlap" (§8.2.2.4).
- **Edge fidelity:** HS/LS matching is internal; the FPGA's edge placement reaches the switch node with only the fixed propagation delay (11 typ / 21 ns max, p.7). IIM would add the 3.4 ns mismatch to every edge.
- **Cost:** the dead time is fixed per build (resistor). Gateware dead-time *compensation* is still possible and required (below).
- The mode is "sampled at power up and cannot be changed during operation" (§7.4). DLH must never be tied to VDD.

## Calculations

### Dead time (Eq. 4, 5; p.7, p.13)
Setting **td = 20 ns → Rext = 900/20 − 25 = 20.0 kΩ** (the datasheet's characterised point: tDEAD_MAX 16 / 20 / 26 ns min/typ/max at Rext = 20 kΩ, p.7). Eq. 5: VDT = 1.8 × 20/45 = 0.80 V → td = (1.8 − 0.80) × 20 = 20 ns ✓. DT pin current 1.8 V/45 kΩ = 40 µA each.
**Effective dead time at the FETs** (review m1): tDEAD is specified with *unloaded* outputs. With IGC033 the outgoing FET turns off in ≈ 8 ns: VGS 5 V → 2.6 V plateau at ≈ 1.2 A through 1.6 Ω sink + 2.2 Ω Rg + 0.5 Ω Rg,int ≈ 3 ns, then Qgd 2.6 nC at 2.6 V/4.3 Ω = 0.6 A ≈ 4.3 ns. The incoming FET reaches Vth ≈ 3 ns after its edge. The window in which neither channel conducts is therefore ≈ td − 8 + 3 = **≈ 15 ns nominal, ≈ 11–21 ns over the 16–26 ns spread**. That is the window the loss model credits (D-gan-fet.md uses 15 ns). It is ≥ 11 ns > 0 at the fast extreme, so there is **no shoot-through**.
Why 20 ns rather than 16 ns: with the 16 ns setting the effective window would be ≈ 11 ns and idle loss +0.8 W (D-gan-fet.md). The larger dead-time error is compensated in gateware and calibrated per board with the post-filter sense, which is fitted on all prototypes (user decision E).
Uncompensated dead-time error per leg at the effective 15 ns: 48 V × 15 ns × 448 kHz = 0.32 V (0.65 V differential, ≈ 0.97 % H3 at 50 W into 8 Ω). Required: gateware compensation (gateware item 4).

### Driver supply current and VDRV load (Eq. 8–10; p.5–6)
Per driver: 2 × Qg(max) 14 nC × fsw + ILSDyn 1.25 mA/MHz × fsw + IHSDyn 0.7 mA/MHz × fsw + IDD 0.55 mA + IHB 0.85 mA + DT 2 × 0.04 mA + gate pull-downs (LS 10 kΩ: 0.5 mA × (1 − D); HS 100 kΩ: 0.05 mA × D) ≈ 0.3 mA:
| fsw | per driver | **4 drivers = VDRV load** | power at 7 V |
|---|---|---|---|
| **448 kHz** | 12.5 + 0.87 + 1.4 + 0.08 + 0.3 = **15.2 mA** | **61 mA** | 0.43 W |
| 512 kHz | 17.0 mA | 68 mA | 0.48 W |
| 672 kHz | 21.9 mA | 88 mA | 0.61 W |
| 1 MHz | 31.7 mA | **127 mA** | 0.89 W |
**VDRV spec: 7.0 V ± 5 %, ≤ 150 mA** (block C's LM5017 is designed for 200 mA). LDO headroom: VIN(min) 6.65 − 5.25 = 1.4 V ≥ 0.75 V max dropout at 100 mA ✓.

### Driver dissipation and Tj
Upper bound 7.35 V × 15.2 mA = 112 mW at 448 kHz (233 mW at 1 MHz), less the gate energy dissipated in Rg. RθJA 40.5 °C/W → ΔTj ≤ 4.5 °C (9.4 °C at 1 MHz). Low-side OTP 150 °C min (§7.3.4) is far away.

### Start-up timing (gateware, block E)
tSTLS 150 µs max after VDD UVLO (PWM mode); tSTHS 28 µs max after HB−HS UVLO (p.7). Bootstrap pre-charges through the internal 1 kΩ (§7.3.1). Sequence: VDRV good → wait ≥ 200 µs → AMP_ARM, latch EN = 1 → PWM low ≥ 50 µs (all LS on: BTL output 0 V, pop-free) → modulation at 50 % (BD idle).

### Minimum pulse and duty clamp (review M1)
In PWM mode the HO and LO pulses are the input pulses "decreased … by the chosen dead-times" (§7.3.3). The LS conducts, and the bootstrap switch is closed, only during LO. **Duty clamp 8–92 % at 448 kHz** → PWM low time ≥ 0.08/448 kHz = 179 ns → LO ≥ 179 − 26 (td max) = **153 ns**. That is the recharge window used in D-bootstrap.md (HB−HS ≥ 4.07 V). The previous 3 % clamp gave a 47 ns LO and HB−HS ≈ 3.6–4.0 V (review M1).
R-50 effect: BD modulation index M_max = 0.92 − 0.08 = **0.84** → 0.84 × 48 V = 40.3 V peak differential → **101 W into 8 Ω** (R-50 "≥ 100 W peak into 8 Ω" ✓), 203 W into 4 Ω (above the 144 W firmware limit, so that limit still binds), 12 A into 3.36 Ω. R-50/R-11's "12 A pk into 4 Ω" means 48 V pk, i.e. M = 1, which was never reachable. Into 4 Ω the current limit at M_max is 40.3/4 = 10.1 A peak at the terminals; the 12 A figure applies to lower impedances (impedance dips below about 3.4 Ω).
Minimum pulse: tPW ≤ 4 ns, pulse extender ≤ 10 ns (p.7) ≪ 153 ns ✓.

## Fine print / gotchas
- "No actual clamp on the high-side bootstrap supply" (§7.3.1): VBST = VDD − VF + IL·Rds(on). Worst case 5.24 V (D-bootstrap.md, review m7).
- DHL/DLH pass an internal 10 kHz RC (§7.3.3): no external capacitance, no switching noise on these pins.
- Two thermal pads on different dies with "very limited thermal coupling" (§8.2.2.5). VSS pad to the GND plane with vias; HS pad to switch-node copper only. "Make the low-side die hotter" (§7.3.4).
- §8.3 Don't: "drive the IC from a controller with a separate ground connection than the VSS pin … unless … CMC". VSS is the GND plane at the LS source (no LS shunt; sensing is in the output path), and the FPGA is on the same plane → 100 Ω series inputs suffice.
- The LMG1210 has no OCP/desat of its own: external (D-current-sense.md, D-short-detect.md, D-ocp-logic.md).
- Rev D raised IHSDyn to 0.7 mA/MHz; used above. MSL2 per the addendum.

## Layout guidance (§10)
Driver as close to the GaN as possible; bootstrap diode as close as possible (small BST–HB loop); VDD–VSS bypass at the pins first, then VIN–VSS and HB–HS; separate power and signal traces; minimise high-side-to-input capacitance; copy Fig. 21.
